The Hotel

A comfortable place to stay in Tuscaloosa

The Rogers Hotel offers quiet, well-kept rooms for visiting families, university guests, and business travelers. Every room is designed around a good night's sleep and an easy stay.

About one-third of our standard rooms feature a king bed, with the remainder offering queen or twin bedding. For longer or special occasion stays, two master suites provide additional space.
