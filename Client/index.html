The House

A hundred and twenty rooms, two suites, and no hurry at all.

Druid City Hotel keeps the pace of an old Southern town: unhurried mornings, long evenings, and a staff who remember how you take your coffee. Three storeys of quiet rooms sit a short stroll from the river, the Strip, and the stadium.

Arrival

Oak, marble, and a warm hello.

Step in from the street and the noise falls away. Check-in is a conversation, not a queue. Your key, a recommendation for supper, and the best route to wherever you are headed.

    The Druid City

    Named for its oaks. Known for its Saturdays.

    Tuscaloosa earned its nickname from the water oaks that line its downtown streets. Stay with us for a graduation, a homecoming, a slow weekend on the Black Warrior River, or a fall Saturday when the whole town turns crimson.
